# User guide

This guide follows the same paths you see in the sidebar.

## Start here

1. Sign in with your username and password (or SSO when your organization turns it on).
2. Open **Home** for what needs attention and short cuts into the app.
3. Use **Help** anytime you need this documentation again.

## People — vacation and onboarding

- **Vacation** — request full days, a half day, or hours. Track status on your request page.
- **Vacation plan** — the 2026 plan for one department at a time. Managers can mark days and accept waiting requests on a day.
- **Availability** — green / yellow / red view of who is in.
- **Vacation calendar** — approved time by month.
- **Onboarding** — dual-check checklists for new hires.

Waiting vacation reviews do **not** live under People. They go to **Approvals**.

## Approvals

**Approvals** is a top-level sidebar item for managers.

- Everything waiting on you: vacation requests and delivery gate reviews.
- A badge shows how many are waiting.
- **Recent decisions** lists decided vacation in your scope; open **Open trail** for the full audit history.

You can also review from your section page; the same waiting list appears there.

## Delivery

- **Road map** — idea → product with Go / No go.
- **Initiatives** — create and steer work through stages.
- **Decisions** — record decisions that unlock the next stage.

## Planning

- Capacity & workload, dependencies & health, portfolio overview, find work, and PI Planning.

## Organization

- Browse structure, sections, and departments.
- Access and admin tools appear when your role allows them.

## Tips for managers

- Prefer **Approvals** as your focus inbox each morning.
- Use the vacation plan for department-level day marks.
- Every vacation decision keeps an audit trail on the request page.
